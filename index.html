TRACKSIDE REPORT: QUANTUM COMPUTING BREAKTHROUGH 📰

Trackside Report brings you the latest report on a groundbreaking development in quantum computing. 🔬💻

Researchers at the University of Surrey designed the Superfluid Helium Oscillator Quantum (SHOQ) qubit.

Utilizing charge-neutral superfluid helium-3, it shields quantum states from electromagnetic noise, potentially reducing error rates by 100 times compared to standard superconducting qubits.

Stay informed and keep up with the latest developments in science and technology! 💻
