01 / ABOUT US

Meet the team.
Find your people.

Experiences that complement the classroom.
Preparation for a future in ICT.

The Junior Philippine Computer Society is an organization of students in Information and Communications Technology. It gives members opportunities to strengthen technical knowledge, develop practical skills, and prepare for the continuous advancement of technology.

See the community Meet the developer (opens in a new tab)
OUR MISSION

Learning beyond the classroom.

To promote learning and active involvement in Information and Communications Technology by providing opportunities for students to expand their knowledge and gain experiences beyond the classroom.

OUR VISION

Progress with purpose.

A progressive and recognized student organization that promotes excellence in ICT education and contributes to the development of competent and responsible future ICT professionals.

TRACKSIDE REPORT: QUANTUM COMPUTING BREAKTHROUGH 📰

Trackside Report brings you the latest report on a groundbreaking development in quantum computing. 🔬💻

Researchers at the University of Surrey designed the Superfluid Helium Oscillator Quantum (SHOQ) qubit.

Utilizing charge-neutral superfluid helium-3, it shields quantum states from electromagnetic noise, potentially reducing error rates by 100 times compared to standard superconducting qubits.

Stay informed and keep up with the latest developments in science and technology! 💻
